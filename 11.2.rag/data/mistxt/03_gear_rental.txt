Baja Trail Hikers Club - Gear Rental

Active members can borrow equipment from the club storage room, which is open on Wednesdays from 14:00 to 16:00. Backpacks can be borrowed for up to five days. Trekking poles can be borrowed for up to three days. Headlamps can be borrowed for up to two days. Every rental requires the club card and a deposit of 100 pesos, which is returned when the equipment comes back in good condition. A late return costs 10 pesos for each extra day.
