Baja Trail Hikers Club - Membership

The Baja Trail Hikers Club is a fictional student organization for people who enjoy hiking. Anyone enrolled at the university can join. The annual membership fee is 150 pesos and it is paid once per school year at the club desk in building C, room 101. Members receive a club card, access to the equipment rental program, and priority registration for weekend trips. Students under 18 need a signed permission form from a parent or guardian. Membership can be cancelled at any time, but the fee is not refunded after the first month.
