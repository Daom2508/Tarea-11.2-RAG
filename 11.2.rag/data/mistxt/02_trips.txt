Baja Trail Hikers Club - Weekend Trips

The club organizes one trip every month, always on a Saturday. Trips leave from the main campus parking lot at 6:30 in the morning and return before 7:00 in the evening. Each trip has a maximum of 20 participants, and registration opens exactly two weeks before the trip date on the club desk sign-up sheet. Members pay 80 pesos per trip to cover transportation and the group first-aid kit.

Trips are classified by difficulty. Level 1 trips are flat walks of about 5 kilometers and are recommended for beginners. Level 2 trips cover between 8 and 12 kilometers with moderate climbing. Level 3 trips are only for experienced members and include more than 12 kilometers with steep sections. Every participant must bring at least two liters of water, sunscreen, a hat, and closed shoes with good grip. A trip leader carries the first-aid kit and has the authority to turn the group around if the weather becomes dangerous. If a trip is cancelled because of rain or heat warnings, the 80 pesos are returned in full.
